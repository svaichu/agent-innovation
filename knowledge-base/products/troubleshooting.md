# Troubleshooting and FAQ

## Sensor shows offline
1. Check in Canopy when the last message arrived.
2. Check the gateway has power and its status light is solid green.
3. Replace the batteries (Sprig) or check the USB-C supply (Sprig Pro).
4. Press the button once; the light should blink green within 30 seconds.
5. If it stays offline, move the sensor closer to the gateway.
6. Still offline: open a ticket with device ID and firmware version.

## CO2 readings look too high or too low
1. Ask whether the room has had fresh air in the last 7 days.
2. Check the sensor is not next to a window, door or people's faces; mount it 1.0 to 2.0 m high.
3. Air the empty room for 20 minutes; the reading should fall below 500 ppm.
4. If it does not, start a return (see the returns process).

## Cannot log in to Canopy
1. Use the password reset link.
2. Single sign-on customers must contact their own IT first.
3. Support can resend an invitation; support never sets passwords for customers.

## Gateway offline
Check network cable and power. The gateway needs outbound HTTPS (port 443). Ask the customer's IT whether a firewall rule changed.

## Frequent questions
**How long do batteries last?** About 4 years at a 10-minute interval; about 2 years at 5 minutes.

**Can I use another brand's gateway?** Sensors work with standard LoRaWAN gateways, but we only support Trellis.

**Where is data stored?** In the EU (Frankfurt).

**Do the sensors record sound or video?** No. They have no microphone or camera.
