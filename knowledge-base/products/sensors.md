# Sensors and gateway: specifications

| Feature | Sprig (BW-S1) | Sprig Pro (BW-S2) |
|---|---|---|
| Measures | CO2, temperature, humidity | CO2, temperature, humidity, PM2.5, VOC |
| CO2 range | 400 to 5,000 ppm | 400 to 5,000 ppm |
| CO2 accuracy | ±(50 ppm + 3% of reading) | ±(40 ppm + 3% of reading) |
| Temperature accuracy | ±0.4 °C | ±0.3 °C |
| Power | 2 AA lithium cells, about 4 years | USB-C |
| Reporting interval | 10 minutes (adjustable 5 to 60) | 5 minutes (adjustable 1 to 60) |
| Radio | LoRaWAN EU868 | LoRaWAN EU868 |
| Current firmware | 3.4.2 | 2.9.0 |
| Warranty | 2 years | 2 years |

## Trellis Gateway (BW-G1)
Ethernet with optional mobile backup. Typically covers one school building or two to three office floors (up to about 200 sensors). Current firmware: 1.8.1.

## Limits
- Our sensors indicate ventilation and air quality. They are not safety or life-protection alarms.
- CO2 sensors self-calibrate and need exposure to fresh air at least once a week.
