# Find the right help centre article faster

## Scope
company process

## Current process
When a ticket arrives I search the help centre by hand and usually open five articles before finding the right one.

## Problem
About 40% of our 1,250 tickets a month are repeat questions, and finding the matching article takes two or three minutes each time.

## Proposed improvement
Suggest the three most likely help centre articles for each incoming ticket, so the specialist can pick one instead of searching.

## Who is affected
The ten support specialists. Customers get a faster first reply.

## Expected benefit
Saves maybe two minutes on 500 tickets a month, so about 16 hours.
