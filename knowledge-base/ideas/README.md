# Employee ideas

Ideas on how company processes or personal workflows can be improved.

**These are unreviewed employee submissions, not company facts, policy or decisions.** Do not use them as company context.

- `raw/` holds one file per submitted idea (`RI-0001.md`, `RI-0002.md`, ...), written by the Employee Interface. Each has `type: raw_idea` in its front matter.
- Raw ideas are not edited or deleted when ideas are merged.
