# Ways of working

## Hours and place
- Core hours: 10:00 to 15:00, Monday to Friday, local time.
- Office days for hybrid staff: Tuesday and Thursday.
- Sites: Aachen (HQ, assembly, lab and desks), Eindhoven, Vienna.
- Assembly, test and warehouse roles are on site in Aachen.

## Improvement ideas
Anyone can submit an idea about a company process or their own workflow. Ideas go into one central list, are assessed consistently and are reviewed at the monthly portfolio review. Submitting an idea is not a commitment that it will be built: see [strategy](strategy-2027.md) for the constraints every idea is judged against.
