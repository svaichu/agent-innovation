# Company overview

Brambleworks GmbH is a **fictional** company with 120 employees, headquartered in Aachen, Germany, with offices in Eindhoven and Vienna. It makes indoor air-quality sensors and the cloud software that monitors them, mainly for schools, offices and light industry.

## Mission
Make the air in shared rooms visible, so people can act before it affects health and concentration.

## Values
