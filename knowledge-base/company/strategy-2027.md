# Strategy and goals 2027

## Priorities
| # | Priority | Target by end of 2027 | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grow recurring revenue | ARR from EUR 4.20 million to EUR 6.00 million | Marco Rossi (E10) |
| 2 | Reduce support load | Tickets per 1,000 installed sensors per month from 13 to 9 | Hannah Fischer (E16) |
| 3 | Launch Sprig Pro 2 | Start of sales in Q3 2027 | Priya Raman (E03) |
| 4 | Shorten delivery time | Median order-to-ship from 8 to 4 working days | Ingrid Solberg (E20) |
| 5 | Use AI where it pays off | Two AI or automation use cases in production, each with a measured benefit | Ottilie Janssens (E26) |
| 6 | Certify the management system | ISO 27001 certificate achieved | Jonas Adler (E02) |

## Constraints
These are the limits any internal project proposal is judged against.

| Constraint | Value |
|---|---|
| Headcount ceiling 2027 | 140 |
| Budget for new internal tooling, including AI | EUR 250,000 |
| Engineering capacity allocated to internal projects | about 60 person-days per month |
| Single project without board approval | up to EUR 20,000 (see [procurement](../policies/procurement.md)) |
| Data residency | customer data stays in EU-hosted systems |
| People decisions | never automated (see [AI usage](../policies/ai-usage.md)) |

The engineering allocation is a deliberate cap, not a measure of free time. Total unallocated capacity across the company is higher, but only about 60 person-days per month of engineering effort is set aside for internal work; the rest is committed to product.

## How internal investment is decided
1. Anyone may submit an idea. Ideas are collected centrally, not per department.
2. Each idea is assessed for overlap, AI suitability, regulatory risk, feasibility against the constraints above, and expected value.
3. The leadership sync reviews the ranked list monthly and selects what is funded.
4. Ottilie Janssens (E26) keeps the portfolio and reports progress against priority 5.
