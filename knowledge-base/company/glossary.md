# Glossary

## Product and operations
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ARR | Annual recurring revenue from Canopy subscriptions. |
| Canopy | Our cloud dashboard and API. |
| CO2 | Carbon dioxide, measured in parts per million (ppm); a proxy for ventilation. |
| PM2.5 | Fine particles smaller than 2.5 micrometres. |
| VOC | Volatile organic compounds. |
| LoRaWAN | Long-range, low-power radio network used by our sensors. |
| Gateway | Device that forwards sensor radio messages to the cloud. |
| OTA | Over-the-air firmware update. |
| RMA | Return merchandise authorisation: the number and process for returned devices. |
| Churn | Subscription revenue lost through cancellation or downgrade. |
| SEV1 to SEV3 | Incident severity levels; SEV1 is the most severe. |
| SLA | Service level agreement. |
| EOL test | End-of-line test: the final functional test before a device ships. |

## Internal projects and idea assessment
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Person-day | One person working one day. The unit for all internal effort estimates. |
| Day rate | The internal cost of one person-day, by seniority. See [capacity.csv](../people/capacity.csv). |
| RICE | A scoring method: (Reach x Impact x Confidence) / Effort. Used to rank internal ideas. |
| Reach | How many people or cases the idea affects in a fixed time period. |
| Impact | How much it improves things per case. |
| Confidence | How good the evidence behind the estimates is. Low confidence lowers the score; it does not hide it. |
| Effort | Total person-days to deliver, including review and rollout. |
| AI suitability | Whether a task needs AI, or whether rules and scripts would do the job. |
| Conventional automation | Rules, scripts, integrations or RPA. No model needed. |
| Overlap | Two ideas that solve substantially the same problem, usually from different departments. |

## EU AI Act terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| EU AI Act | Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 on artificial intelligence. |
| Prohibited practice | A use banned outright under Article 5, for example inferring emotion of workers. |
| High-risk system | A use listed in Annex I or Annex III and classified under Article 6; needs conformity work before deployment. |
| Annex III | The list of high-risk areas. Point 4 covers employment and worker management. |
| Transparency obligation | Article 50: people must be told when they interact with an AI system. Applies in addition to any risk class. |
| Minimal risk | No specific obligation. Recorded only when the information supports it. |
| DPIA | Data protection impact assessment, required before new large-scale processing of personal data. |
