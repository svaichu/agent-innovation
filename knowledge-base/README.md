# Brambleworks knowledge base

> All companies, people, customers and figures here are **invented** for testing and demonstration.

Brambleworks GmbH makes indoor air-quality sensors and monitoring software. 120 employees across Aachen (HQ), Eindhoven and Vienna. Content reflects the state on 2026-11-26.

## Conventions
- Every page starts with `title`, `owner`, `classification` and `last_updated`.
- `classification` is enforced: `public`, `internal`, `confidential`. A tool reading this knowledge base must show an employee only what their role allows.
- People are referenced by ID (E01 to E120); look them up in the [directory](people/directory.md).
- Money is in EUR, excluding VAT. Dates are YYYY-MM-DD. Effort is in person-days.
- Tables with a CSV twin: employees, capacity, systems, data inventory, key accounts.
