# Lead to order

**Process owner:** Marco Rossi (E10)

**Starts when:** a lead arrives through the website, an event or a referral.

## Steps
1. A sales development representative qualifies the lead in HubSpot within 1 working day (DACH: E13, E64; Benelux: E69).
2. The lead is routed to an account executive by region and segment (DACH education: E11, E60, E61; DACH commercial and industry: E12, E62, E63; Benelux: E66, E67). The account executive holds a discovery call and demo.
3. For more than 100 sensors, a field installation technician does a site survey (E19, E91 to E94).
4. The account executive sends a quote, valid for 30 days. Discounts follow the [price list](../products/catalog.md).
5. On acceptance, the account executive marks the deal won and attaches the signed order.
6. Operations receives the order and starts [order fulfilment](order-fulfilment.md).
