# Employee onboarding and offboarding

**Process owner:** Fatima El-Amrani (E25)

**Starts when:** a contract is signed, or notice is given.

## Steps
1. E25 creates the person in Personio and names an onboarding buddy.
2. E09 prepares laptop and accounts at least 3 working days before the start.
3. Day 1: security and data protection briefing.
4. Week 1: read this knowledge base and meet your manager and deputy.
5. Check-ins after 30, 90 and 180 days.
6. Offboarding: E09 disables accounts on the last day; E25 collects equipment.
