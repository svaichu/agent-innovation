# Incident response

**Process owner:** David Okafor (E09)

**Starts when:** monitoring, staff or a customer reports an outage or security problem.

## Steps
1. The first person to notice posts in the Slack channel #incidents and calls E09 (backup E02).
2. E09 sets the severity.
3. The incident lead fixes or contains the problem; E16 informs customers.
4. If personal data may be affected, tell E23 immediately: authorities may need notice within 72 hours.
5. Write a review within 5 working days for SEV1 and SEV2.

## Severity
| Level | Definition | First update to customers |
|---|---|---|
| SEV1 | Canopy down, or confirmed data breach | within 1 hour |
| SEV2 | Major function broken for many customers | within 4 hours |
| SEV3 | Limited problem with a workaround | not required |
