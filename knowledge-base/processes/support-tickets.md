# Support tickets

**Process owner:** Hannah Fischer (E16)

**Starts when:** a customer emails, calls or uses the help centre form.

## Steps
1. The request becomes a Zendesk ticket.
2. E17 or E18 sets category, priority and plan.
3. Try the [troubleshooting guide](../products/troubleshooting.md).
4. If engineering is needed, create a Jira issue with device ID, firmware version, steps and logs.
5. Reply to the customer, and close after confirmation or 7 days without reply.

## Response targets
| Priority | Example | Canopy Pro | Canopy Standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Urgent | All sensors at a site offline | 1 working hour | 4 working hours |
| Normal | One sensor faulty | 4 working hours | 1 working day |
| Low | How-to question | 1 working day | 2 working days |

Support hours: Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00.
