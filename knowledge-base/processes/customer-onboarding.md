# Customer onboarding

**Process owner:** Hannah Fischer (E16)

**Starts when:** the first shipment to a new customer leaves.

## Steps
1. E16 assigns a support specialist.
2. The specialist creates the Canopy account and invites the customer's administrator.
3. E19 installs on site if booked.
4. Training session within 2 weeks of delivery.
5. Check-in after 30 days: all sensors online and at least one active user.
