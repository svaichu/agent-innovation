# Order fulfilment

**Process owner:** Ingrid Solberg (E20)

**Starts when:** a signed order is received.

## Steps
1. E21 enters the order in the inventory spreadsheet and checks stock.
2. Missing parts are ordered following [procurement](../policies/procurement.md).
3. E22 assembles, flashes firmware and runs the end-of-line test.
4. E21 ships and sends tracking details to the customer.
5. E24 issues the invoice on the shipping date; payment term is 14 days.
6. E16 starts [customer onboarding](customer-onboarding.md).
