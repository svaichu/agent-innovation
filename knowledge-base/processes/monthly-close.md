# Monthly close

**Process owner:** Miriam Scholz (E23)

**Starts when:** a calendar month ends.

## Steps
1. E24 collects supplier invoices and expense claims by working day 3.
2. E24 books them in DATEV and reconciles bank accounts.
3. E21 reports the stock count.
4. E23 reviews accruals and prepares the report.
5. The books currently close on working day 9 (target 5); the close review follows on working day 10.
