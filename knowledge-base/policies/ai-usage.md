# Use of AI tools

## Allowed
- The company AI assistant (business plan, no training on our data) for drafting, summarising, translation and code help.
- Public and internal information in that assistant.

## Not allowed
- Personal data or customer contracts in any AI tool without approval from E23.
- Private AI accounts for company work.
- Decisions about people (hiring, performance) made by AI.
- Sending AI-generated text to customers without a human reading it.

## Proposing a new AI use case
1. Describe the problem, users, expected value, data and effort.
2. E03 checks for overlap with existing ideas and projects.
3. E02 and E23 check feasibility, security and data protection.
4. The leadership sync decides. Tools may recommend; people decide.
