# Leave and working time

- 30 days of paid vacation per year for full-time staff.
- Request vacation in Personio at least 2 weeks ahead; the manager decides within 3 working days.
- Up to 5 unused days carry over until 31 March.
- Sick leave: tell your manager before 09:30. A medical certificate is needed from the fourth calendar day.
- 40-hour week with flexible time; record working time in Personio.
- Learning budget: EUR 1,200 and 5 days per person per year.
