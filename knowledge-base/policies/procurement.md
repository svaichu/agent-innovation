# Procurement and approvals

| Value (EUR, net) | Who approves | Extra requirement |
|---|---|---|
| up to 1,000 | Employee | Keep the receipt |
| 1,001 to 10,000 | Department head | Purchase order |
| 10,001 to 50,000 | Department head and Miriam Scholz (E23) | Two quotes |
| over 50,000 | Katharina Vogel (E01) and Miriam Scholz (E23) | Three quotes |

## Internal projects and tooling
Internal improvement projects, including AI use cases, are funded from the internal tooling budget of EUR 250,000 for 2027 (see [strategy](../company/strategy-2027.md)).

| Total project cost (EUR, net) | Who decides |
|---|---|
| up to 20,000 | Department head, reported to the leadership sync |
| over 20,000 | Leadership sync, on the ranked portfolio |

Project cost means external spend plus internal effort at the day rates in [capacity.csv](../people/capacity.csv).

## Always required, whatever the price
- New software that processes **personal data**: approval from Miriam Scholz (E23), Jolanda Esposito (E113, Data Protection Officer) and David Okafor (E09).
- A **data processing agreement** before any processor is used.
- Anything that would place customer data outside the EU: refused.
