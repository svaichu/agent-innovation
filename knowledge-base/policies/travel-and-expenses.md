# Travel and expenses

- Prefer rail; flights only if the rail journey is longer than 6 hours.
- Hotel limit: EUR 130 per night in Germany, EUR 170 abroad.
- Second-class rail.
- Submit claims with receipts within 30 days; E24 pays with the next salary run.
- Meals are reimbursed as statutory daily allowances.
- Anything above a limit needs the manager's approval in advance.
