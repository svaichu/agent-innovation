Our solution

AI agents prepare the ideas, people decide

1. Clarify

Intake agent

  • Employee describes an idea in a chat
  • Asks about process, problem, change and numbers
  • Employee checks the draft and submits it

2. Score and rank

Score agent

  • Scores each new idea with RICE, using company knowledge
  • Flags AI fit and EU AI Act risk
  • Dashboard ranks ideas and shows reasons

3. Merge

Merge agent

  • Runs when the innovation team presses a button
  • Combines related ideas from different employees
  • Each refined idea lists its sources
