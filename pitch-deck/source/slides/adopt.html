Why adopt it

Less effort to contribute, less chasing to review

For employees

  • No form to fill in: just describe the problem in a chat
  • Nothing is submitted until you press the button
  • Come back and improve your idea; earlier versions are kept

For the innovation team

  • Every idea has the same six fields, so they can be compared
  • Gaps are asked about before the idea reaches you
  • One dashboard across departments instead of scattered messages

For trust

  • Collecting an idea is kept apart from judging it
  • The agent uses only what the employee said
  • Employees see only their own ideas in the chat

The agent recommends. People decide.
