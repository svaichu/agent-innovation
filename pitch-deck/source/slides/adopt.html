Why adopt it

Clearer ideas now, fewer separate reviews next

For the innovation team

  • Today: ideas arrive with the same six fields, in one dashboard
  • Planned: one review covers a group of related ideas

For employees

  • Describe your own workflow in a chat, with no form
  • Nothing is submitted until you press the button

For the company

  • Vague wishes become concrete proposals
  • Planned: problems shared across departments become visible
  • The innovation team can spend its time on decisions
